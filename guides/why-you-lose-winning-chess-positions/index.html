Why you lose winning chess positions

When the evaluation is favourable, ask what the opponent can still do. Identify checks, forcing captures, passed pawns, and active pieces; then choose a plan that keeps your advantage while reducing those resources. The simplest conversion is often more reliable than the most tactical.

A conversion checklist

What is the opponent’s only active idea? Which trade helps me? Which trade removes my best piece? Can I improve my worst-placed piece? If I make a quiet move, what forcing reply appears?

Review the first loss of control

Do not start with the final blunder. Find the earlier move where the winning plan became harder, counterplay appeared, or clock usage accelerated. That decision is usually the better coaching position.
