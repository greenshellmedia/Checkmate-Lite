How to stop hanging your queen in chess

The most reliable way to stop hanging your queen is to make queen safety part of every move, not only queen moves. Before committing, check opponent checks, captures, and threats; trace every attack on the destination square; then ask what became undefended when the queen moved.

A three-question queen safety check

1. Is my queen attacked now? 2. What forcing reply follows my intended move? 3. Which piece or square stops being defended after the queen moves? Say the answers before releasing the piece.

Practise the trigger, not only the tactic

Review each queen loss and tag the trigger: missed long-range attack, overloaded defender, discovered attack, back-rank tactic, or simple destination-square oversight. Repeated triggers make better practice themes than the generic label “blunder.”
