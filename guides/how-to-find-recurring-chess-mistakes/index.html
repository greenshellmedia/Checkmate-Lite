How to find mistakes that keep coming back

Start with critical positions from a useful sample. Label the underlying cause: missed forcing move, unsafe piece, opening-plan confusion, rushed decision, or poor king activity. Then compare how often the opportunity occurred and whether the same response repeated.

From blunder labels to useful themes

“Blunder” describes severity. “Ignored an opponent capture after moving the same piece twice” describes a trainable habit. Recurring-pattern work should move from the first label to the second explanation.

When is a habit corrected?

Do not call a habit fixed after one good game. Compare a later sample with the earlier baseline and require enough eligible opportunities before presenting improvement as sustained.

Try this

Save one representative position, name the decision rule you want to remember, and review whether it appears again across your next few suitable games.
