How to Review Your Chess Games

A useful chess-game review starts with your own decisions. Replay the game without an engine, record what you expected, identify positions where you felt uncertain, and only then compare those ideas with engine-backed analysis. Finish with one or two actions you can practise.

A repeatable review process

First pass: mark decisions and emotions. Second pass: calculate alternatives at critical moments. Third pass: check classifications, evaluation swings, and best lines. Final pass: write one tactical and one strategic takeaway only if the evidence supports both.

Questions worth asking

When did the plan become unclear? Which opponent threat did I miss? Did I know the opening idea or only the move order? Was the endgame decision technical, tactical, or caused by time pressure?

What to do next

Save one representative position, name the decision rule you want to remember, and review whether it appears again across your next eligible games.
