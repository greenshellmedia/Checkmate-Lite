From games to a coaching loop

How CheckmateMore turns your games into chess training.

CheckmateMore brings recent Chess.com games into the browser, processes them with engine-backed analysis, and organises the results into explanations, profile trends, weekly objectives, and practice positions. The value is the full loop: analyse, understand, find recurring themes, practise, then review progress.

Four steps from game to practical next action

Bring in your games

Link a Chess.com profile and initiate an import from the browser. CheckmateMore stores processed games in your account library so they can contribute to future reviews.

Explain the critical moments

Engine evaluation, move classifications, material events, tactical ideas, phase scores, and best alternatives are combined into a readable game story where available.

Find recurring patterns

Profile trends and weekly coaching compare already-analysed games. Continuing problems, emerging themes, and corrected habits require enough evidence before they are presented as patterns.

Practise what matters

Suitable tactical misses become personal practice positions. Repeated themes are prioritised and spaced repetition brings missed positions back sooner.

Why context matters beyond an engine number

A raw evaluation tells you which side an engine prefers. Coaching context asks a different question: what happened, why was the practical decision difficult, has the same theme appeared before, and what should you practise next?

Move context

Classifications, evaluation swings, material events, tactical ideas, and alternatives are explained together.

Weekly review

One to three objectives keep the plan focused and evidence-based.

Practice queue

Critical positions from your own games return on a spaced schedule.

Bots, play-yourself, and privacy

Human-tendency bots

Configured bots can combine nominal strength with situation-based tendencies. Personal-style bots are an evolving beta concept and are not claimed to mimic a real person perfectly.

Your account and data flow

Normal Chess.com game import is initiated in your browser. Processed games can be stored in your CheckmateMore account; curated featured profiles may be refreshed separately. See the public privacy page for the current summary.

Frequently asked questions

Is CheckmateMore Pro in beta?

Yes. Features and availability may evolve while the platform is being expanded. The site describes current beta capabilities without promising that every feature appears in every account at all times.

Does CheckmateMore promise rating improvement?

No. CheckmateMore provides analysis, coaching objectives, and practice tools. It does not guarantee wins or a particular rating outcome.
