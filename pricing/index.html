Pricing FAQ

What is the difference between Lite and a free account?

Lite reviews one game in the browser and does not store account history. A free account stores analysed Chess.com and Lichess games so later coaching, openings, and puzzles have a library to use.

Why is annual pricing lower?

Annual Pro is £10 instead of 12 × £1, which saves £2. Annual Unlimited is £50 instead of 12 × £5, which saves £10.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Billing and cancellation are handled in the authenticated app. Review the checkout terms shown there before you subscribe.

Which plan should I choose?

Use Lite for a one-off review. Create a free account when you want memory. Add Pro for weekly coaching, personal training and Improvement Proof. Unlimited adds opponent preparation and private profile-derived bots.
