Honest beta positioning

Features may evolve during beta.

Not every capability is universally available in every account at all times. The app shows current plan entitlements, pricing, renewal details, and any eligible promotion before you upgrade. For plan or billing questions, email support@checkmatemore.com.

Frequently asked questions

Why is annual pricing lower?

The annual Pro and Unlimited prices equal ten monthly payments, effectively including two months free.

Can I cancel?

Billing and cancellation are managed in the authenticated app. Review the checkout terms shown there before subscribing.

Which plan should I choose?

Free is for discovery and basic use. Pro is for personal weekly coaching and more detailed analysis. Unlimited is aimed at advanced opponent preparation and private bot capabilities.

Is CheckmateMore Pro in beta?

Yes. Features and availability may evolve while the platform is being expanded. The site describes current beta capabilities without promising that every feature appears in every account at all times.
