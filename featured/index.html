Featured-player FAQ

Do I need an account to browse featured players?

No. The featured gallery is public. Some full game or coaching experiences may invite visitors into a CheckmateMore account.

Does a featured profile represent every career game?

No. Findings are based only on the processed game corpus available to CheckmateMore and are not a complete record of a player’s career.
