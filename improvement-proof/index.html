A complete journey from habit to evidence.

Lesson completion shows that you studied. Improvement Proof asks the harder question: did that work change a decision you made over the board?

  1. 01

    Detect the habit

    Recurring misses are grouped across analysed games. CheckmateMore establishes a measurable baseline and chooses one high-impact focus.

  2. 02

    Train the pattern

    Work with positions from your own games and related Stockfish-validated variations, included in your daily training plan.

  3. 03

    Apply it in real games

    Keep playing normally. CheckmateMore watches future games for moments where the same habit matters.

  4. 04

    Prove the improvement

    Compare relevant opportunities before and after training, with context and an evidence-based state.

What the evidence looks like

Confidence built on repeated decisions, not one good result.

The measurement follows the habit wherever it appears. Relevant games can be compared by opening, game phase, rating range and time control so the result is useful without pretending every position is identical.

Improvement Proof FAQ

What is Improvement Proof?

It is CheckmateMore's way of connecting analysis, focused practice and later games. It establishes a baseline for a recurring habit, recommends one high-impact focus, and measures whether later decisions show that the habit is changing.

Does one good game count as proof?

No. A single performance can be encouraging, but Improvement Proof is based on repeated relevant opportunities. It considers context such as openings, phases, ratings and time controls before reporting a state.

Where do the practice positions come from?

Practice begins with suitable positions from your own analysed games. Related positions are generated and validated with Stockfish so you learn the underlying pattern rather than memorising one move.

Which plans include Improvement Proof?

Improvement Proof is available with Pro and Unlimited. A free account stores analysed games and helps build the history needed for later measurement. Lite remains a free, no-account single-game review.

Does a Strong state guarantee rating gain?

No. It means the available relevant games show strong evidence that a specific trained habit has changed. Chess results and ratings are affected by many other decisions.

Pro and Unlimited are in beta. Improvement Proof features, thresholds and availability may evolve as the evidence model is refined. The app shows current entitlements before you upgrade.
