Compare approaches

Game review and coaching memory solve different problems.

Chess.com is a full chess platform. CheckmateMore is narrower: remember patterns across stored games, choose a short weekly plan, and practise eligible positions from those games. The two can sit together.

Where the work differs

Four questions, two useful answers.

What happened in this game?

A one-game review on Chess.com or in CheckmateMore can show evaluation, alternatives, and the critical moment. CheckmateMore then keeps that review in a library when you use an account.

What should I work on this week?

Chess.com offers a wide learning catalogue. CheckmateMore Pro selects one to three objectives from your stored sample, with evidence positions and a success condition.

What should I practise?

Use Chess.com puzzles if you want a broad tactics diet. CheckmateMore builds a queue from suitable misses in your own games.

How do bots behave?

That depends on each platform. CheckmateMore can combine a strength label with situation-based tendencies, including play-yourself on Pro.
