Talk to the CheckmateMore team.

Use the address that matches the request. Product and billing questions go to support. Privacy requests go to privacy. General and editorial notes go to hello.

Choose the right inbox

Four addresses, four jobs.

hello@checkmatemore.com

General enquiries, partnerships, editorial questions, and featured-profile requests.

support@checkmatemore.com

Product, account, subscription, and billing help. Include the page, browser, and a short description. Do not publish private account information.

privacy@checkmatemore.com

Data access, correction, deletion, and other privacy requests.

noreply@checkmatemore.com

Automated service messages only. Replies to this address are not monitored.
