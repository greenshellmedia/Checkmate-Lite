What to do next

Frequently asked questions

How are practice positions chosen?

Eligible positions come from analysed critical moments in the player’s own games. Repeated tactical themes can be prioritised, while unsuitable or unclear positions may be excluded.

What revisit schedule is used?

The practice model uses expanding 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30-day intervals. Missed positions return quickly; correct positions move to a longer interval.

Does CheckmateMore promise rating improvement?

No. CheckmateMore provides analysis, coaching objectives, and practice tools. It does not guarantee wins or a particular rating outcome.
