Questions

Practice FAQ

How are practice positions chosen?

Eligible positions come from analysed critical moments in the player’s own stored games. Repeated tactical themes can be prioritised. Unsuitable or unclear positions may be excluded.

What revisit schedule is used?

The practice model uses expanding 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30-day intervals. Missed positions return quickly. Correct positions move to a longer interval.

Is this available in Lite?

Lite reviews one game and does not store a practice queue. A free account can start personal puzzles from stored games. Pro adds the fuller weekly coaching loop around those positions.

Pro and Unlimited are in beta. Practice depth and availability may evolve with your plan. The app shows current entitlements before you upgrade.
