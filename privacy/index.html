CheckmateMore privacy policy

This policy explains how GreenShellMedia, trading as CheckmateMore, handles personal information when you use our sites, chess-analysis tools, accounts, subscriptions, and public profiles.

Effective date: 27 August 2026

1. Who is responsible for your information

GreenShellMedia, trading as CheckmateMore (we, us, or our), is the controller of personal data handled through CheckmateMore Pro. Our registered address is Derby, UK. Contact us at privacy@checkmatemore.com.

This policy explains how we use personal data when you visit our sites, create an account, link a chess account, import games, buy a subscription, use a public profile, or contact us.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use CheckmateMore, we may collect:

  • Account and contact data: name, email address, account username, sign-in provider identifier, and account settings.
  • Authentication data: the identity information returned by Microsoft or Google when you choose to sign in. We do not receive your Microsoft or Google password.
  • Chess data: linked Chess.com or Lichess usernames, public game records you ask us to import or are permitted to provide, PGN/moves, game metadata, ratings, results, and platform identifiers.
  • Analysis and learning data: engine evaluations, move classifications, estimated performance ratings, coaching observations, puzzles, bot settings, saved positions, annotations, and profile statistics derived from game data.
  • Subscription and transaction data: plan, billing status, subscription dates, Stripe customer/subscription references, promotion-code redemption, and payment outcome. Stripe handles payment-card data directly.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser/device information, logs, error reports, and information needed to prevent fraud, protect the Service, and diagnose faults.
  • Communications data: messages and support requests you send us, and records that a platform email was sent to you.

3. Where information comes from

We collect data directly from you, from your use of the Service, from your sign-in provider, from Stripe for billing events, and from supported chess platforms when you authorise a link or import publicly available game data.

We may also derive new information from data we hold, such as an opening trend, coaching objective, puzzle, or estimated game performance. Derived analysis is still treated as data about the relevant user or player where it can be linked to them.

5. Public profiles and chess data

CheckmateMore can display public player dossiers built from analysed chess games. These are limited analysis samples. They do not claim to represent a player's entire record, or that a player has an account, approved a profile, or endorses CheckmateMore.

For featured or monitored profiles, we may retain and analyse a broader public-game corpus so that the public dossier, recent games, opening statistics, and permitted coaching notes work. We use this data to operate the public-profile feature and our legitimate interest in maintaining a chess-analysis catalogue. We assess removal or correction requests case by case; contact privacy@checkmatemore.com.

6. Who we share information with

We share personal data only where needed to run the Service, including with:

  • Stripe, which processes subscription payments and provides billing infrastructure.
  • Microsoft and Google, where you choose those sign-in providers.
  • Chess.com and Lichess, where you link an account, request an import, or use their public-game data through the Service.
  • Hosting, database, email, analytics, security, and support providers that process data for us under appropriate contractual safeguards.
  • Authorities, professional advisers, or other parties where required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or the Service.

We do not sell personal data.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where that happens, we will use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards required by applicable data-protection law.

8. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, including to provide the Service, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and maintain security.

Our current intended retention approach is:

  • account, linked-account, game, analysis, coaching, and subscription records: while your account remains active, then removed when space is required unless a longer period is required by law or justified for security/dispute handling;
  • email delivery logs: up to three months;
  • routine operational and error logs: up to three months;
  • public-profile corpus data: while the relevant public-profile feature is maintained, subject to review or removal where appropriate.

We may retain anonymised or aggregated information that no longer identifies you.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to ask us for access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain processing. You may withdraw consent where we rely on consent. You can also opt out of marketing at any time using the link in the message or by contacting us.

To make a request, email privacy@checkmatemore.com from the email address connected to your account. We may need to verify your identity. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Deleting an account does not automatically remove data that must be retained for legal, security, or accounting reasons, or public game data that is not uniquely tied to your account. We will explain the scope of any deletion request.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary storage and technologies to keep the Service working, secure sign-in, remember settings, and preserve session state. We will present consent choices before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies where required by law.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please protect your sign-in account and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised use.

12. Children

CheckmateMore is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate permission, contact privacy@checkmatemore.com and we will review the request.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service or legal requirements change. We will publish the new version with an updated effective date and provide additional notice where appropriate.
