About CheckmateMore

Chess analysis that remembers how you play.

CheckmateMore helps adult improvers review their own games, understand critical decisions, find patterns that repeat and practise positions that matter.

More than a basic engine analysis board.

Review the game

Stockfish-backed analysis checks the position and identifies important changes.

Explain the decision

Move context, plans and human-tendency analysis help describe why a choice was difficult where eligible.

Connect later games

Repeated evidence can turn a single mistake into a focused training objective.

How the parts connect

A game review is useful on its own. CheckmateMore becomes more useful when several reviews form a longer record. Recurring decisions can inform weekly objectives and personal practice positions. Improvement Proof then looks for relevant opportunities in later games and reports whether the evidence is early, mixed, insufficient or sustained.

CheckmateMore does not promise rating gains and does not replace a human coach. It organises engine-backed evidence and practical training material from the games a player chooses to analyse.

Who it is for

CheckmateMore is for chess players who want to understand their own decisions rather than collect another general list of tips. Lite supports a one-game browser review. A free account keeps eligible analysed games and adds a library, puzzles and starter coaching. Paid plans add broader coaching and analysis features as described on the current pricing page.

Who operates CheckmateMore

CheckmateMore is operated by Green Shell Media in Derby, United Kingdom. Product questions can be sent to hello@checkmatemore.com, support requests to support@checkmatemore.com, and privacy requests to privacy@checkmatemore.com.
