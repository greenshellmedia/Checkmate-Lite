Personalised weekly coaching

A weekly chess plan grounded in the games you played.

CheckmateMore weekly coaching reviews already-analysed games and selects one to three practical objectives. Each objective can include evidence positions, model answers or best lines, and a measurable success condition, helping players focus on recurring themes rather than an overwhelming list of generic tips.

From one-off review to a coaching loop

One game

A review explains critical decisions in a single game. It is useful immediately, but it cannot establish a recurring habit by itself.

A weekly sample

The coaching review compares games with a baseline, separates continuing and emerging patterns, and only marks habits corrected after sufficient evidence.

What a weekly objective contains

Evidence positions

Clickable positions show where the theme appeared in your own games.

Model answer

A best line or practical answer demonstrates the preferred decision where available.

Success condition

A measurable condition makes the objective reviewable next week.

ILLUSTRATIVE WEEKLY PLAN

Objective: check forcing captures before moving the queen

Evidence: two positions where an attacked piece was left undefended. Practice: solve the saved positions and state checks, captures, and threats before moving. Success condition: no repeat queen hang in the next eligible five-game sample.

Five coaching areas, one focused plan

Objectives can draw from tactical, strategic, opening, endgame, and time-management evidence. The system chooses a small number of priorities so a player can act on them before the next review.

Frequently asked questions

Does CheckmateMore replace a human coach?

No. It organises engine-backed evidence and practical objectives from your games. A human coach can still add judgement, accountability, and broader teaching context.

Is CheckmateMore Pro in beta?

Yes. Features and availability may evolve while the platform is being expanded. The site describes current beta capabilities without promising that every feature appears in every account at all times.

Does CheckmateMore promise rating improvement?

No. CheckmateMore provides analysis, coaching objectives, and practice tools. It does not guarantee wins or a particular rating outcome.
