A worked weekly objective

What you actually get for the week.

Illustrative Pro plan

Check forcing captures before moving the queen

Evidence: two stored positions where the queen was left en prise. Model: the capture that would have been played. Practice: the same boards in personal puzzles or a play-yourself bot. Success condition: no repeat queen hang in the next eligible five-game sample. This example is not a claim about a named player.

Objectives can draw from tactics, strategy, openings, endgames, and time use. The plan stays short so you can act before the next review.

Limits

What weekly coaching does not claim

Does this replace a human coach?

No. It organises engine-backed evidence and a short plan from your stored games. A human coach can still add judgement, accountability, and broader teaching context.

Is weekly coaching in Lite?

No. Lite reviews one game in the browser. Starter coaching can appear on a free account. Weekly objectives with evidence and success conditions are a Pro feature.

Does it promise a rating gain?

No. A focused week is still practice from your own games, not a guaranteed rating outcome.

Pro and Unlimited are in beta. Features and availability may evolve. The app shows current entitlements before you upgrade.
