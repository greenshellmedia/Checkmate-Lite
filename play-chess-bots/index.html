Bots and play

Chess bots with tendencies, not only an ELO label.

CheckmateMore bots can combine a nominal playing strength with configured situation-based tendencies such as tactical oversights, hanging pieces, overmoving pieces, or missing mates. Community and personal-style bot capabilities are being expanded during the beta; no bot is claimed to reproduce a real person perfectly.

What makes a bot feel more human?

A bare engine strength setting mainly controls move quality. A tendency-aware bot can also change how errors occur: overlooking tactics in certain situations, moving the same piece repeatedly, hanging material, or missing a mating opportunity. These are configured behaviours, not a psychological model of a real player.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play against my own chess style?

Play-yourself is a developing concept that can derive a private bot from eligible profile tendencies over time. Availability depends on plan and beta rollout.

Do bots perfectly mimic a person?

No. They can use configured or profile-derived tendencies, but they do not reproduce a real person perfectly.

Is CheckmateMore Pro in beta?

Yes. Features and availability may evolve while the platform is being expanded. The site describes current beta capabilities without promising that every feature appears in every account at all times.
