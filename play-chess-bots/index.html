Bots with tendencies

Chess bots with tendencies, not only a strength label.

A bare engine setting mainly changes move quality. CheckmateMore bots can also be given situation-based habits, such as hanging a piece or missing a mate. No bot is claimed to reproduce a real person perfectly.

What each plan is for

Entry-level, play-yourself, then private preparation.

Free account

Entry-level bots

Audience: players who want a first practice opponent after storing games.

Outcome: configured sessions with a nominal strength and some human-like mistakes.

Pro

Play-yourself

Audience: players with enough analysed games to see their own habits.

Outcome: a private bot that can use eligible profile tendencies as the beta develops.

Unlimited

Private profile-derived bots

Audience: players preparing for a specific opponent style.

Outcome: private bots and opponent preparation on top of Pro coaching, as they become available.

Questions

Bot FAQ

Can I play against my own chess style?

Play-yourself is a developing Pro concept that can derive a private bot from eligible profile tendencies over time. Unlimited adds further private profile-derived bots and opponent preparation. Availability depends on plan and beta rollout.

Do bots perfectly mimic a person?

No. They can use configured or profile-derived tendencies, but they do not reproduce a real person perfectly.

Does Lite include these bots?

Lite is an in-browser game review. Bot play lives in the CheckmateMore account, starting with entry-level bots on the free plan.

Pro and Unlimited are in beta. Personal-style bots are still being expanded. The app shows current entitlements before you upgrade.
