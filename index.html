What we do

Game review that talks like a coach, not a spreadsheet.

Paste a Chess.com username. We pull your recent games, run an engine pass in the browser, and build a profile of how you actually play — not a generic tip dump.

  • Move quality you can trust the shape of Expected-points labels aligned with modern review (Best → Blunder), plus our Book / Theory split so openings and famous games stay distinct.
  • Form across many games Accuracy trends, Game ELO estimates, phase CPL, punish rates, piece habits, and heatmaps — so one blunder doesn’t define the story.
  • Coaching with receipts Theme tags, material events, and clickable evidence moves. Learn openings and theory lines from the same catalog we use to classify your games.

How we do it

Local Stockfish. Your data stays with you.

Lite is fully client-side: Chess.com’s public API for games, Stockfish in a web worker for analysis, and localStorage for your cache. Depth presets from Lite Rapid to IM & GM let you trade speed for sharpness.

  • Parallel game scans Multiple engine workers chew through new games while already-analyzed ones load from cache at the depth you chose.
  • Critical moments, not noise On deeper presets we re-search sharp swings; Familiar stays closer to what other online engines feel like day to day.
  • Privacy by architecture No Checkmate More account for Lite. Analysis never leaves your browser session’s machine.
