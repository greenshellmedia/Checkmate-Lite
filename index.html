The Pro experience

A coaching system built around how you play.

Bring your games together, find the ideas that recur, and turn them into practical work for the week ahead.

01

Connected game library

  • Import and organise your analysed games
  • Search, filter, sort, save filters, and revisit key games
  • Track accuracy, estimated game strength, blunders, openings, and results over time
  • Review opening-book context, theory alternatives, and leave-book moments
02

Personal coaching

  • Weekly coaching reviews built from your own games
  • One to three practical objectives with evidence positions
  • Model answers, best lines, and measurable success conditions
  • See recurring habits, emerging issues, and improvements that have held over enough games
  • Tactical, strategic, opening, endgame, and time-management themes
03

Deeper game analysis

  • Multi-depth engine analysis and richer move context
  • Game plans, strategies, attacks, defences, tendencies, and human explanations
  • Endgame insights including king activity, passed pawns, rook activity, opposition, pawn majorities, and conversion technique
  • Clock-data insights when the source game provides timing information
  • Save annotations, bookmarks, critical positions, arrows, and highlights
04

Progress and profile insights

  • Weekly rating, accuracy, and phase-score trends
  • Opening, middlegame, and endgame performance over time
  • Opening and variation strengths and weaknesses based on how you actually play
  • Coaching dossiers and deeper profile analysis where available
  • Public and external-player profile exploration
05

Practice and bots

  • Play against preset bots
  • Coach mode with suggested moves and explanations
  • Play critical positions again to practise a better continuation
  • Your personal playing-style bot as the product develops

More than a verdict

Why Pro instead of a one-off engine review?

A single review can tell you where a move went wrong. CheckmateMore Pro looks for the patterns behind it: the openings that repeatedly cause trouble, the plans you execute well, the habits you are correcting, and the positions worth practising again.
