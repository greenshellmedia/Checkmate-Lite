CheckmateMore FAQ

What is the difference between CheckmateMore Lite and a free account?

Lite is a free chess game review that runs Stockfish in your browser. No account is required, and Lite does not store personal data as account history. A free account stores analysed games, connects Chess.com and Lichess, and adds a game library, public profiles, starter coaching, puzzles, and entry-level bots.

Can CheckmateMore analyse Chess.com and Lichess games?

Yes. Lite can review an individual Chess.com game in the browser. Create a free account to connect Chess.com and Lichess and keep eligible games for later analysis, opening study, and coaching.

How does CheckmateMore help you improve at chess?

It analyses your own games, explains critical moments, looks for chess mistakes and patterns that keep coming back, then turns suitable positions into personal practice or a short weekly plan. It supports an improvement process but does not guarantee a rating outcome.

Does Lite store my games or account history?

No. Lite reviews the game in front of you. If you want a library and later comparisons, create a free CheckmateMore account.

What do Pro and Unlimited add?

Pro adds weekly coaching, one to three evidence-backed objectives, progress tracking, opening, middlegame and endgame trends, personal practice, playing-style analysis, play-yourself bots, and deeper public-profile analysis. Unlimited adds private profile-derived bots and opponent preparation.

Is a single-game performance estimate my real rating?

No. It is an estimate based on the rated decisions in one game, shown separately from the source rating with Low, Moderate, or Strong sample confidence. It is not your Chess.com, Lichess, FIDE, or over-the-board rating.

Is CheckmateMore a replacement for a human chess coach?

No. It organises engine-backed evidence and clear objectives from your games. A human coach can still add judgement, accountability, and broader teaching context.
