CheckmateMore FAQ

How does CheckmateMore help you improve at chess?

It analyses your own games, explains critical moments, looks for recurring patterns, creates one to three weekly objectives, and turns suitable positions into personal practice. It supports an improvement process but does not guarantee a rating outcome.

Can CheckmateMore analyse Chess.com games?

Yes. Link a Chess.com profile and initiate eligible game imports from your browser. Normal user imports are not described as automatic server-side polling.

What is a single-game performance estimate?

It is an estimate based on the rated decisions in one game, shown separately from the game’s source rating with Low, Moderate, or Strong sample confidence. It is not your Chess.com, FIDE, or over-the-board rating.

How are personal practice positions chosen?

Suitable positions come from analysed critical moments in your own games. Repeated themes can be prioritised, and spaced repetition brings missed positions back sooner.

Is CheckmateMore free?

There is a Free account plan for profiles, openings, basic analysis and coaching, game caching, and entry-level bot play. Pro and Unlimited are paid beta plans for deeper coaching, history, training, and preparation.

Is CheckmateMore a replacement for a human chess coach?

No. It organises engine-backed evidence and practical objectives from your games. A human coach can still add judgement, accountability, and broader teaching context.
