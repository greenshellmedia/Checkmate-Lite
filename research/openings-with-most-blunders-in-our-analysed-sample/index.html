Method, limitations, and learning takeaway

Planned method

Run a moderated aggregate query, verify classification logic and deduplication, inspect representative positions, and publish both counts and denominators. Segment only where every group meets a disclosed minimum sample.

Current limitations

Without a verified corpus size, date range, and query output, any percentage or ranking would be fabricated. Selection effects will remain even after publication because analysed users and featured profiles are not a random sample of all chess players.

Learning takeaway

Use the eventual pattern to build a position-recognition habit, not to stereotype a rating band, opening, or player.
